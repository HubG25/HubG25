Disclaimer: this tab's baseline Fit % is the exact NNLS least-squares solution on all G25 dimensions. Single and Multi admixture instead use a fast, stochastic Monte Carlo optimizer (their cycles setting) that approximates that same solution rather than solving it directly, so their weights and distance can come out slightly different — usually by a fraction of a percent. Raising cycles in Single/Multi narrows the gap at the cost of speed.
